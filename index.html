Selected work

  • Predicting maize yield, pixel by pixel

    Master's thesis, University of Padova

    At Ca' Bianca, a 21-hectare reclaimed farm near Chioggia, I combined a crop growth model (EPIC), soil maps I built with Kriging, and satellite data, and used them as inputs to machine-learning models that predict yield across the field. I tested every model on a season it had never seen, which turned out to matter a lot. A paper is in preparation with my supervisors.

    Why it matters: in this area, pumping groundwater for irrigation makes the land sink. Knowing what each part of a field will produce is a first step to irrigating only where it's needed.

    EPIC, Kriging, Sentinel-2, Random Forest, XGBoost, SHAP, Python, R

  • Hot and dry at the same time in the Po Valley

    Personal project

    Are heatwaves and droughts arriving together more often in northern Italy? I took 75 years of daily ERA5 climate data for six cities, from Milano to Rovigo, and counted the days that were both very hot and very dry. Those days roughly doubled after 1990, and the increase is significant in five of the six cities.

    See the code on GitHub

    Python, ERA5, Mann–Kendall trend test

  • How much rain is a 200-year storm?

    Personal project

    Engineers size drains and levees with rare rainfall estimates. I compared two methods for estimating the 200-year daily rainfall, the classic GEV and the newer MEVD, and measured how much each estimate shifts and how uncertain it is over moving 30-year windows.

    See the code on GitHub

    R, extreme value statistics, bootstrap

  • Hydrology of alpine basins

    Research fellowship, from October 2026

    At the TESAF department in Padova, with Prof. Marco Borga, I'm working on better ways to simulate how water moves through mountain catchments.
